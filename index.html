Privacy Policy for Calisma

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Calisma (“the app”) is provided by Pierre Moggee (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information the app uses

To build your workout plan, the app asks for your age and either your height and weight or your BMI. The app also remembers which workout rounds you have ticked off.

Where your information is stored

All of this information is stored only on your device, inside the app. We do not have user accounts, we do not run servers that receive your information, and we cannot see it. Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tools.
  • We do not track your location or access your contacts, photos or microphone.

Third-party links

Each exercise has a “Watch a demo video” link. Tapping it opens YouTube in your browser, which is operated by Google and has its own privacy policy. We have no control over information collected there.

Deleting your information

You can change your details at any time with “Edit details” in the app. To delete everything the app has stored, clear the app’s storage in your Android settings or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Users under 18 should check with a parent, coach or doctor before starting a new exercise routine.

Health disclaimer

The app provides general fitness guidance and is not medical advice. BMI is a rough guide and not a diagnosis. If you have a health condition or injury, or are unsure whether an exercise is safe for you, speak to a doctor before you start.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
